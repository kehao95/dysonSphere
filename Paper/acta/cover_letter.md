# Draft Cover Letter

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, "Low-latitude displaced non-Keplerian support for stratified Dyson swarm architectures," for publication in *Acta Astronautica*.

This paper studies low-latitude displaced non-Keplerian equilibria supported by solar-radiation-pressure-like forcing and frames them within a broader continuum of sail-supported space architectures. The main contribution is not the introduction of a fundamentally new orbit family, but a systematic extension of displaced-orbit design space toward lower latitudes together with an architectural interpretation that makes these equilibria useful for screening and conceptual design.

We believe the manuscript fits *Acta Astronautica* because it combines astrodynamics, space-system architecture, and forward-looking mission analysis in a way that is technically grounded while remaining relevant to broader space-engineering audiences.

The work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors approve its submission. We have included data/code availability and model-boundary statements to support reproducibility and clarify the scope of the analysis.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Hao Ke
